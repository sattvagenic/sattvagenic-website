Privacy

What this site and Nadi collect about you, why, where it's kept, for how long, and how to have it removed. Last updated 31 October 2026.

Who we are

Sattvagenic is run by Andrew Jepson, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your information.

Address: 7 Chapel St, Longridge, Lancashire PR3 3JH
Email: hello@sattvagenic.com

What we collect, and why

  • Your email address, when you download Nadi. We use it to tell you about the software you downloaded, such as a new version or an important fix. We do this because it's in both our interests that you can keep using what you downloaded.
  • Sattvagenic news, only if you ask for it. If you tick the box on the download form, we'll also send the occasional news about new plugins, releases and music. You can unsubscribe from any of these emails, or just tell us, and we'll stop.
  • Purchases. Nadi is sold through Gumroad, which takes the payment and sends us your name, email address and what you bought, so we can provide your licence and support. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers what it does with your card details.
  • Activating Nadi. When you enter your licence key, the plugin sends that key to Gumroad to check it. Nothing else about you or your computer is sent.
  • Emails you send us. We keep them to answer you.

We never sell or share your information with anyone else for their own use.

Where it's kept

The site is hosted by Netlify, which receives your IP address when you visit, to deliver the pages, and stores what you type into the download form. Netlify keeps this in the United States, protected by the data-protection terms in Netlify's contract with us.

The site has no analytics and no tracking or advertising cookies, and its fonts are served from this site.

A few pages show content from other services:

  • YouTube videos load only when you press play, and they use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • The music players on the home and Music pages come from Bandcamp, which receives your IP address when those pages open.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address while you might still need updates for what you downloaded, and for no longer than five years after your last download or purchase, unless you ask us to remove it sooner. Purchase records are kept for as long as UK tax rules require (about six years).

How to be removed, and your rights

To be removed, email hello@sattvagenic.com with "remove me". We'll delete your email address within a month and confirm when it's done. You can also unsubscribe from the news with the link in any of our emails.

You can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or object to how we use it. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
