    Key Features

    • Seven chakra resonators, each a distinct material body
    • Individual volume, pan and decay per resonator
    • Raga-inspired, customisable just-intonation tuning
    • The shakti — a rhythmic energy source exciting the resonators

    • MIDI input to play the resonators melodically

    Chakra-Inspired Resonance

    Nadi draws on the three main energy channels of yogic philosophy — Ida, Pingala and Sushumna. Together, they balance and energise the seven resonant centres.

    Each resonator is modelled on a sacred material: temple stone, bell metal, crystal singing bowl and more. Together they shape frequency into something that resonates on a deeper level.
